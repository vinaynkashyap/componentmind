Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 21, 2026

ComponentMind ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy and minimizes data collection by design. Our browser-based Live Component Audit Sandbox executes 100% client-side inside your browser—component code pasted into the sandbox is not transmitted to external servers.

1. Information We Collect

2. Data Sharing & Selling

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or source code to third parties.

3. Data Security & Contact

For data access, deletion requests, or security questions, contact founder@componentmind.com.
