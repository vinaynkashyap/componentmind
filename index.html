    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is ComponentMind?

    ComponentMind is a developer-first UI component intelligence and autonomous auditing engine that scans React, Next.js, and Astro repositories to index components, catch WCAG accessibility and Core Web Vitals regressions, and expose your design system to AI coding agents over MCP.

    How is ComponentMind different from Storybook or Chromatic?

    Storybook focuses on manual isolated component previews and Chromatic focuses on visual snapshot testing. ComponentMind requires zero story boilerplate, runs static semantic + CWV + GEO audits inside CI in under two seconds, and gives coding agents live MCP access to your component graph.

    Does ComponentMind require a credit card to get started?

    No. The Developer Open tier is free forever with no credit card required.
